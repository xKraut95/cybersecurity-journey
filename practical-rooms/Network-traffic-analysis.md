## What I learnt:

- The importance of analyzing network traffic in different network environments, corporative or familiar local network.
- Checking different logs and their variables to analyse and make a fast decision in different situations.
- The importance of software like Firewall, IDS, IPS or AD domains to protect the internal network against threats.
- The different types of scanning, Vertical, Horizontal, External and Internal Scans.
- Different types of scans, Ping scans, Handshake scans and UDP scans.
- The importance of gatekeeping the network perimeter to protect important assets.
- Different type of attacks using the network traffic.
